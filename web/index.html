Luma calendars

Paste links to the Luma calendars you follow. In the Luma app open a calendar, tap Share, then Copy link. You can paste several at once, or a whole message with links in it.

    Import everything I follow in one go

    Luma only shows the list of calendars you follow when you are logged in, so this needs your browser.

    Bookmarklet. Save this link as a bookmark, then open it while on luma.com. It reads your followed list and sends you back here with it. On iPhone: bookmark any page, then edit the bookmark and replace its address with the copied text.

    Import Luma follows

    Or, on a computer, sign in at luma.com, open DevTools → Application/Storage → Cookies → luma.com, copy luma.auth-session-key, and paste it here. This also keeps the list in sync as you follow new calendars.

    Mark the Luma events I'm going to

    Optional. In Luma go to Settings → Calendar Syncing → Add iCal Subscription, pick Google Calendar, and copy the link it opens (it contains your personal feed). Paste it here and those events show with a ✓.

    Partiful

    Partiful gives every account a personal calendar feed. On any Partiful event tap the calendar icon, choose Google Calendar, then Copy Link, and paste it here. It covers events you are invited to, going to, or hosting.
