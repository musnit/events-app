Luma

Luma has no way for another app to ask "who does this person follow", so a one-time bookmark does it from your own logged-in browser. Tap it once on luma.com and every calendar you follow lands here. Setup takes about a minute.

  1. Tap Copy bookmarklet below.
  2. Tap Safari's Share button → Add Bookmark → Save. (It bookmarks this page; that's fine.)
  3. Open Bookmarks (book icon) → Edit → tap the new bookmark → clear the Address field and paste. Tap Done.
  4. Go to luma.com/home, make sure you are signed in, then open Bookmarks and tap that bookmark. You will land back here with everything imported.
  1. Tap Copy bookmarklet below.
  2. In Chrome, tap ⋮ → ☆ to bookmark this page, then ⋮ → Bookmarks → ⋮ on it → Edit. Replace the URL with what you copied and name it "Import Luma".
  3. Open luma.com/home signed in, tap the address bar, type Import Luma, and pick the bookmark from the suggestions.
  1. Drag the Import Luma follows link below onto your bookmarks bar (or copy it and create a bookmark with it as the address).
  2. Open luma.com/home signed in and click the bookmark.
Import Luma follows

    Add single calendars by link

    Paste one or more luma.com calendar links. In the Luma app: open a calendar → Share → Copy link.

    Paste a Luma session cookie (computer only)

    Sign in at luma.com, open DevTools → Application/Storage → Cookies → luma.com, copy luma.auth-session-key, paste it here. The followed list and your RSVPs then sync on their own.

    Mark the Luma events I'm going to

    Optional. In Luma go to Settings → Calendar Syncing → Add iCal Subscription, pick Google Calendar, and copy the link it opens (it contains your personal feed). Paste it here and those events show with a ✓.

    Partiful

    Same idea as Luma: a one-time bookmark tapped on partiful.com hands this app your Partiful login. It then reads the events from people and orgs you follow, plus your own upcoming events, and keeps them fresh on its own.

    1. Tap Copy Partiful bookmarklet below.
    2. Safari Share → Add Bookmark → Save, then Bookmarks → Edit → tap it → replace the Address with the copied text.
    3. Open partiful.com/events signed in, then open Bookmarks and tap it.
    1. Tap Copy Partiful bookmarklet below, bookmark this page in Chrome, then edit that bookmark's URL to the copied text and name it "Import Partiful".
    2. Open partiful.com/events signed in, type Import Partiful in the address bar and pick the bookmark.
    1. Drag Import Partiful below to your bookmarks bar, open partiful.com/events signed in, and click it.
    Import Partiful

    Or just the calendar feed link

    Covers only events you are invited to or going to. On any Partiful event tap the calendar icon, choose Google Calendar, then Copy Link, and paste it here.
